# Stochastic heat equation — Lean formalization

Lean 4 formalization accompanying *A Counterexample to Single-Control Null Controllability of Stochastic Heat Equations* by Feizheng Huang and Zhaobo Liu.

[Download the Lean verification archive](https://github.com/guohaoji21/stochastic-heat-lean/releases/download/proofs-2026-11-25/StochasticHeat_Lean_20261125.zip) · [Release notes](https://github.com/guohaoji21/stochastic-heat-lean/releases/tag/proofs-2026-11-25) · [Browse the verified sources](https://github.com/guohaoji21/stochastic-heat-lean/tree/eb36cac854a3160f1edba61af74fdacf0c8e12ef)

The code-only archive contains Lean sources, pinned dependencies, rebuild instructions, and verification logs. It covers the one-dimensional Dirichlet counterexample and the periodic Gevrey corollary.

The Lean 4.35.0 audit checked 6,885 declarations in 517 project modules. The axiom dependencies were limited to `propext`, `Classical.choice`, and `Quot.sound`.
